In short

  • We collect what we need to answer requests, deliver written replies and take payment.
  • Conversations (requests, follow-ups and our replies) are kept separately, seen only by us and our consultants, and deleted 90 days after the conversation closes.
  • For the long term, we keep only an anonymized service log: a short, cleaned-up summary of each question with no names, contact details or company names, plus administrative details such as dates and tier.
  • We never log the content of out-of-scope requests.
  • We don’t sell your information, show ads, or use your conversations to train AI models.
  • Questions or deletion requests: prompthumanhq@gmail.com.

Who this covers

PromptHuman (“we”, “us”) connects AI agents with real people for short, paid questions answered in writing. This policy explains how we handle personal information across everything we do: our website, our MCP server and JSON API, the requests we receive, the replies we write and the payments we take.

“You” means the operator of the requesting agent (the person or company that runs it), including when the agent contacts us on your behalf. We treat requests from an agent as coming from its operator. This policy works alongside our Terms, including their confidentiality section. Where the two differ, the one that gives your information more protection applies.

What we collect

Requests and conversations. Agents submit requests only through our MCP server or JSON API. We receive the question, any background, the tier chosen, follow-up messages, and technical details needed to run the service, such as when each message arrived. We write replies that we return to your agent through the same channel.

Email. If a person emails us with a question about the service or a refund, we receive the sending email address and the message.

Payments. Requests are paid by card through Stripe. Through Stripe we receive your email address, your name or business name if you provide one, your billing country and postal code, the request reference you enter at checkout, and the amount and status of the payment. We never see or store full card numbers.

Phone and voicemail. If you call or text our business number, we receive your number and any message you leave.

Our website. Our website is a static site hosted by GitHub Pages. We don’t use cookies, analytics or tracking scripts. GitHub, as our host, may log technical information such as visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, under GitHub’s privacy statement.

What we ask you not to send. As our Terms say, never send passwords, API keys, tokens or other credentials; payment card, bank account or government ID numbers; health or medical records; personal information about anyone other than the operator; or information you are not authorised to share. Our server rejects obvious cases automatically, and we may decline or close a request that contains such information and delete it without answering.

How we use it

  • To decide whether a request is in scope and reply to it.
  • To write and deliver replies and follow-up replies.
  • To take payments, issue refunds and keep accounting and tax records.
  • To keep the anonymized service log described below, which we use to improve our service.
  • To prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms and meet legal obligations.

We don’t sell personal information, use it for advertising, or use conversations to train AI models.

How we store and delete it

We keep three kinds of records, each stored and kept for a different period.

Record What it contains How long we keep it
Conversations Requests, background, follow-ups and our replies Deleted 90 days after the conversation closes
Payment records Payment and refund details held by Stripe (under its own policies) and in our accounts As long as tax and accounting law requires [for example, seven years]
Service log Anonymized summaries and administrative details Kept indefinitely

Conversations travel over encrypted connections and are stored with our hosting provider, Cloudflare, separately from the service log. Only we and our consultants can read them. Our phone notifications about new requests contain no request content. We keep a conversation past 90 days only if we need it to resolve a dispute, refund or chargeback, or if the law requires us to keep it, and we delete it once that need ends.

Your agent’s platform may keep its own copy of the conversation. That copy is your responsibility, and deleting ours doesn’t affect it.

The service log

To understand what agents need and improve our service, we keep a log of every request. It contains:

  • a short summary of the question, written by us, with names, contact details, company names and identifying specifics removed;
  • a request ID and a client code, which let us recognize repeat clients without recording who they are;
  • administrative details, such as how the agent found us, the agent platform, the type of operator (company, individual or unknown), the question category, the tier and price, the dates and times a request was received, reviewed and answered, the number of replies and words used, payment and refund status, and whether our answer changed the agent’s next step.

The log never contains raw request text, names, email addresses, phone numbers, company names or payment details. If we decline a request as out of scope, the log records only that it was declined and the general reason, such as “medical”, never what the request said.

The key that links a client code to a client is held only in our conversation and payment records, which follow the retention periods above. We may share aggregate statistics from the log, such as how many requests fall into each category, but never in a way that could identify a client or a specific request.

Who we share it with

  • Our consultants, who have each signed an agreement with us that includes confidentiality terms. They see only what they need to handle your request.
  • Service providers who store or process information for us: Cloudflare (our MCP server, JSON API and conversation storage), Stripe (payments), Google (email and our business phone number) and GitHub (website hosting). Each handles information under its own privacy policy.
  • Legal and safety reasons, when we believe in good faith that the law requires it, or to protect the rights, property or safety of PromptHuman, our clients or others.
  • A business transfer, if PromptHuman is merged, sold or reorganized. Anyone who takes over must protect your information at least as well as this policy does.

We don’t share or publish conversations with anyone else.

Information about other people

A request may mention people other than you, such as your users or colleagues. You’re responsible for having the right to share their information with us. We treat it the same way as yours: it stays in the conversation record, it’s deleted 90 days after the conversation closes, and it never enters the service log.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we hold about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that’s wrong;
  • delete your conversations before the 90 days are up;
  • stop using your information for a particular purpose.

Email prompthumanhq@gmail.com and include your conversation ID so we can confirm the request is yours. We’ll respond within 30 days. We may need to keep payment records the law requires us to keep. The service log contains no personal information, so there is nothing in it to hand over or delete.

Security

We protect our accounts with two-step verification, encrypt conversations in transit, keep them separate from the service log, limit access to us and our consultants, and delete them on schedule. No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we’ll notify you as the law requires.

International clients

We’re based in Pennsylvania, in the United States, and our service providers store information in the United States and elsewhere. If you contact us from another country, your information is transferred to and handled in the United States.

If you’re in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, we rely on these legal bases: performing our agreement with you (handling requests, replies and payments), our legitimate interest in improving our service (the anonymized service log) and in preventing fraud, and legal obligations (tax and accounting records). You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Our service is for businesses and adults. We don’t knowingly collect information from anyone under 18.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top. If a change materially affects how we handle information we already hold, we’ll highlight it on this page before it takes effect.
